# Gemini art prompts — "grimy dungeon"

Rules for every image:
- Original art only: no Dungeon Crawler Carl characters, logos, or cover art.
- Dark, desaturated stone and soot, with warm torchlight (amber, about #E8A33D) as the only strong color. A little moss green is fine.
- No text or letters in the image (Gemini mangles them, and the app draws its own text).

Save finished images to `Downloads\DCC Books\Crawler Codex\art\` on the PC with the file names below, then tell Claude.
Claude crops/resizes them and wires them into the app.

---

## 1. App icon — `icon.png` (square, 1:1)
> A square app icon: a thick, battered leather-bound tome lying closed on wet dungeon flagstones, a single
> glowing amber rune pressed into its cover, lit by one torch from the upper left. Heavy shadow, soot,
> scratched iron corner caps. Centered, bold silhouette that reads clearly at tiny sizes. Painterly
> dark fantasy, desaturated, high contrast, no text.

## 2. Header banner — `banner.png` (ultra-wide, 21:9)
> An ultra-wide panoramic view down a long, grimy underground stone corridor, rows of flickering wall
> torches receding into darkness, damp moss in the mortar cracks, a faint glowing doorway far at the end.
> Very dark overall with warm amber torch glow, cinematic, painterly dark fantasy, empty of people, no text.
> Keep the important detail in a thin horizontal band across the middle.

## 3. Background — `background.png` (tall phone, 9:16)
> A subtle, very dark texture of ancient dungeon stone wall, uneven blocks, soot stains, faint moss, a
> hint of warm torchlight bleeding in from the top edge. Low contrast and mostly near-black so text stays
> readable on top of it. No objects, no people, no text, seamless and calm.

## 4. Type icons (square, 1:1) — one image each
Shared style line (paste at the end of each):
> …as a single bold, chunky icon, centered on a pure black background, carved-stone-and-brass look with
> warm amber rim light, very simple shapes that stay readable at 18 pixels, no text.
