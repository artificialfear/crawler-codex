# Changelog

## 1.3.1 — 2026-11-26

## 1.3.0 — 2026-11-26
- Each book has its own color (spot box, Browse sections, chapter tags, last seen).

## 1.2.0 — 2026-11-26
- Custom art: dungeon-corridor header banner, stone-wall background, new app icon, and illustrated icons for each entry type.

## 1.1.0 — 2026-11-26
- New look: grimy-dungeon theme with system-message cards, a terminal font, and a color for each entry type. Parchment light mode.
- Bottom tabs: **Now** (your party + this book so far), **Browse**, **Recent**.
- Names inside notes are tappable and jump to that entry (only names you've already met).
- Every card shows when you last saw them ("last seen B6 · Ch 8").
- Browse is grouped by the book where you first met each entry, with type filters.
- Pin anyone with ☆ to keep them in Your Party (starts with Carl, Donut, Mordecai, Katia).
- Recent shows what either of you logged lately — never anything past your spot.
- Silly achievements. Spots ready for custom Gemini art.

## 1.0.0 — 2026-11-26
- First version of Crawler Codex.
- One box to search or add: type a name, tap Add, done. Book and chapter fill in from your spot.
- Big "Next ch" button to keep your spot current; tap your spot to jump anywhere.
- Each person only sees what's at or before their own spot. Emily's later notes stay hidden from Mat.
- Close-spelling matches for names you only heard (Katya → Katia Grim).
- Nicknames, "mentioned here" logging, editing, and tagging a note to an earlier spot.
- Starter entries for Books 1–4, marked so Emily can check them off.
- Setup link to configure the second phone in one tap. Light/dark mode. Keep-screen-awake option.
- Works offline: changes save on the phone and sync when it can.
